THE PEOPLE & PURPOSE BEHIND THE MOSAIC

Technology, with
humanity at its heart.

Humanitarian Tracker connects citizen knowledge with technology, crowdsourcing and AI for social good.

Community is
where it begins.

Humanitarian Tracker is a volunteer-powered nonprofit that connects and empowers communities through technology, crowdsourcing and artificial intelligence. Its work spans humanitarian reporting, health, education and sustainable development.

Through Global Action Mosaic, the organization helps community-led initiatives gain visibility, share knowledge and connect with others working toward the Sustainable Development Goals.

Independent of political parties and religious affiliations, Humanitarian Tracker brings people together around a shared purpose: using local knowledge to support humanitarian action.

Listen.

Local contributors share experiences and knowledge through text, photographs and video.

Corroborate.

Reports are checked against other citizen contributions and official sources to build a clearer picture.

Connect.

Mapping and analysis bring individual contributions into a picture that others can explore.

Care for the people
behind the data.

Humanitarian Tracker’s approach puts people at the center of the information they share. Its work emphasizes protecting contributors’ identities, checking reports carefully and being transparent about the limits of crowdsourced data.
