Epidemic Tracker ( استبيانات الأوبئة )
During a crisis, an increase in disease outbreaks can be exacerbated by the breakdown of infrastructure and waste disposal. It is also believed that conducting surveillance, while essential, can be extremely difficult especially during times of war. However, there is still a need to contain the outbreaks.
Sample questionnaires
General purpose Tracker (also available in Arabic ) is a rapid collection form to help clinicians track epidemics on the ground (such as Tuberculosis ( السل ), Leishmaniasis ( الليشمانيا ), Viral Hepatitis ( التهاب الكبد الفيروسي ), among other epidemics.
